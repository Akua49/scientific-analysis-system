# Survey S-1 Questionnaire

## Survey S-1 (Google Forms, 23.10.2026, N = 26)

Anonymous online survey of AITU students and academic staff. The form was published under the working name SciCheck. Raw responses: [survey-S1.csv](survey-S1.csv).

- Q1. What is your current role? (undergraduate student; master’s student; PhD student; university lecturer or professor; other)
- Q2. How often do you check references and citations in academic documents? (always; often; sometimes; rarely; never)
- Q3. How do you usually check references and citations in scientific documents? (manually; open links and verify sources individually; reference management software; plagiarism detection software; other digital tools; do not usually check; other)
- Q4. How much time do you usually spend checking references and citations in one scientific document? (less than 15 min; 15–30 min; 31–60 min; more than 1 hour; depends on the document; do not perform these checks)
- Q5. What is the most difficult part of checking an academic document? (broken links or incorrect DOIs; whether sources actually support citations; abstract accuracy; compliance with academic requirements; similar research topics; other)
- Q6. Have you ever encountered broken links or references that could not be verified? (yes; no; not sure)
- Q7. What tools do you currently use to check academic documents? (Turnitin or similar; DOI or reference lookup services; reference management tools; manual checking; other)
- Q8. Which features would be most useful to you in such a system? (link check; DOI and bibliographic metadata; citations support statements; abstract vs. content; academic requirements; similar research topics; report with detected issues; other)
- Q9. What additional features or improvements would you suggest? (open question)
